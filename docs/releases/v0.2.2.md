## The embed fits a column

`<pk-table>` mounted in a book for the first time (bower EPIC-19, *Rust for
Failures*), in a 750px content column inside a wide window, and the seat
plates at the left and right edges clipped. The compact-seat layout was keyed
on the window's width; it is now keyed on the table's own width with a
container query. In a narrow host the embed shows the phone layout, every
plate inside the frame. The main site, where the table is as wide as the
window, draws exactly as before.

- `www/css/table.css`: `@container (max-width: 759px)` on `#table-zone` and
  `.pk-zone`.
- `tests/embed.spec.ts` 6b pins it: a 750px host, no plate outside the zone.
- One flaky assertion in the rule-3 test awaited.

No game logic, bot data or difficulty behaviour changed. Consumers re-fetch
the tarball and its `MANIFEST.sha256`; nothing else moves.
